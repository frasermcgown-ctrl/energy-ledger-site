Understanding the NBP gas price

What is the NBP gas price?

The National Balancing Point is the virtual trading hub for natural gas in Great Britain. Gas bought and sold anywhere on the national transmission system is treated as traded at the NBP, so its price is the benchmark for UK wholesale gas.

What is the System Average Price?

SAP is the volume-weighted average price of gas traded on the on-the-day market for each gas day (6am to 6am). It is a published cash price, not a forward contract, so it can differ from the day-ahead, month-ahead and seasonal prices quoted by brokers.

How do I convert pence per therm to p/kWh?

One therm is 29.3071 kWh, so divide the price in pence per therm by 29.3071. For example, 100p/therm is about 3.41p/kWh. Business and household unit rates are higher because they include network, policy and supplier costs.

What moves UK gas prices?

Norwegian pipeline flows, LNG cargoes, North Sea output, storage levels, weather and wind all matter. UK gas also trades closely with the Dutch TTF hub, so European supply news and geopolitical risk feed straight into NBP.

Why does the gas price matter for electricity?

Gas power stations often set the GB wholesale electricity price, so a rise in NBP usually pushes power prices up too, especially on low-wind days.
