Data sources

Every figure and headline on this site comes from an official or openly licensed source. Here is what we use, how often it updates, and how it is licensed.

Market data

National Gas Transmission Data Portal ↗

NBP System Average Price (SAP), gas supply by terminal, demand, linepack, storage flows, daily storage stock levels and UK LNG terminal flows

Updated
Hourly (prices), every 3 hours (flows), twice daily (storage stocks)
Licence
Published under National Gas's Gas System Operator licence obligation for open data

Elexon Insights Solution (BMRS) ↗

GB power market index price, national demand and its forecast, wind generation forecast, and metered wind and solar output (used to check the renewables weather outlook against what happened)

Updated
Hourly (prices), every 3 hours (forecasts)
Licence
BMRS Data Licence

Contains BMRS data © Elexon Limited copyright and database right 2026.

NESO Data Portal ↗

Grid balancing costs (BSUoS cost data), embedded wind and solar forecasts

Updated
Daily (costs), every 3 hours (forecasts)
Licence
NESO Open Data Licence

Contains data from the National Energy System Operator (NESO).

Live carbon intensity, generation mix and 48-hour carbon intensity forecast

Updated
Live, on each page load

Carbon Intensity API, National Energy System Operator.

Weather

Met Office ↗

7-day forecasts for coastal and inland sites near Britain's main wind and solar areas (Wick, Dunbar, Bridlington, Lowestoft, Barrow-in-Furness, Llandrindod Wells and Cambridge), plus the shipping forecast, UK 10-day and long-range outlooks and weather warnings. These feed the renewables weather outlook.

Updated
Weekly, every Monday morning
Licence
Open Government Licence v3.0 (Met Office website terms of use). The wind and solar ratings are our own judgement and are not produced or endorsed by the Met Office.

Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Forecast information from the Met Office.
