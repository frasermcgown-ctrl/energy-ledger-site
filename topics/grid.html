Grid and balancing costs

Network constraints, balancing costs and the transmission build-out, and who pays for them.

When wind farms in Scotland are switched off and gas plants in the south paid to run, the cost lands on bills through balancing charges. These issues follow NESO's balancing costs and the grid upgrades meant to bring them down.
