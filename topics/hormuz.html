Hormuz and the Middle East

How tension around Iran and the Strait of Hormuz feeds into UK gas, power and oil prices.

Around a fifth of the world's LNG and oil passes through the Strait of Hormuz, including Qatari LNG cargoes bound for Europe. When the risk of disruption rises, traders price it into gas and oil straight away, and UK business energy costs follow. These issues track that risk premium as it builds and fades.
