Price cap and business bills

The Ofgem price cap, government policy and what they mean for business energy bills.

The Ofgem price cap only covers households, but the same wholesale, network and policy costs drive what businesses pay. These issues explain each cap change, Budget measure and policy shift, and what it means for anyone buying energy for an organisation.
