Understanding UK power prices

What is the market index price?

Elexon's market index is the volume-weighted average price of electricity traded for delivery in each half hour on the short-term exchange market. The figure here is the daily average of the 48 half-hourly prices, in £ per megawatt hour.

Why does gas set the power price?

Gas power stations are usually the last plant needed to meet demand, so their running cost often sets the wholesale price. When gas gets dearer, power usually follows.

How do I convert £/MWh to p/kWh?

One MWh is 1,000 kWh, so divide by 10. A wholesale price of £100/MWh is 10p/kWh. Bills are higher because they also cover networks, balancing, policy costs, VAT and supplier margin.

Why do prices swing so much from day to day?

Wind output changes fast. On windy, sunny days with low demand, renewables push prices down, sometimes below zero. On still, cold evenings, expensive gas plant and imports fill the gap and prices jump.

Is this the UK or Great Britain price?

Great Britain. Northern Ireland is part of the all-island Single Electricity Market with Ireland, which has its own wholesale price.
